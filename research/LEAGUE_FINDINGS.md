# League findings

Generated by `python -m research.run_league` from the **dev** dataset: 7 series, 643 consecutive-game transitions, 19 primary offensive players (120 transitions).
Seasons: 2024-25, 2025-26.

> **Development sample.** With fewer than 20 series, intervals are wide and
> these numbers show the pipeline working, not league conclusions. Run
> `python -m scripts.fetch_playoffs` locally, then rerun this script.

All significance is model-based (see `FINDINGS.md`): a *robust* adjustment has
league q < 0.10 on both the time and possession noise bases. Intervals
resample whole series (cluster bootstrap). Nothing here identifies coaching
intent; see `FACE_VALIDITY.md`.

## 1. How often do matchup changes survive the noise baseline?

- Primary offensive players: 18% (95% CI 6% to 32%) of transitions are robust.
- Non-primary players: 17%.
- Of 26 large raw changes (TVD ≥ 0.30) for primary players, 62% survive.
- Excluding blowouts: 19% (95% CI 5% to 39%) (n = 80).

## 2. Overlap or assignment?

Share of summed TVD in robust, well-covered transitions of primary players:

| Component | All games | Excluding blowouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overlap / rotation | 39% (95% CI 33% to 49%) | 39% (95% CI 25% to 52%) |
| Assignment | 53% (95% CI 45% to 59%) | 51% (95% CI 42% to 72%) |
| Entry / exit | 8% (95% CI 4% to 13%) | 10% (95% CI 2% to 15%) |

## 3. Do adjustments follow losses?

- Robust-adjustment rate when the defending team lost the earlier game: 20%; after a win: 17%.
- Difference (loss − win): 3 pp (95% CI -5 pp to 10 pp).
- Excluding blowouts: 8 pp (95% CI -4 pp to 14 pp).

## 4. What happens to the offensive player next game?

Regression of the later game's deviation from the player's own series
average on a robust-adjustment indicator, controlling for the earlier
game's deviation (regression to the mean). Association only.

- Points per 36: -1.6 (95% CI -5.1 to -0.1); excluding blowouts -1.5 (95% CI -5.9 to 4.4).
- True shooting: -1.4 pp (95% CI -5.0 pp to 2.6 pp); excluding blowouts -2.5 pp (95% CI -6.9 pp to 6.8 pp).

## Definitions

- Primary offensive player: ≥ 28 minutes per game played and team top-2 in usage per game (FGA + 0.44 FTA + turnovers), from box scores.
- Blowout: final margin ≥ 20 in either game of the transition (33% of primary transitions).
- Low decomposition coverage (< 0.80): 6% of primary transitions; excluded from the component shares.
- Transitions across games a player missed are excluded.
